# SalesMax v2 demo · Thu Nov 12, 12:00 PM
Test copy: https://f2gxwmm5hjzcrnncfizyfmyewe0jzevo.lambda-url.us-east-2.on.aws/  (sign in with your email + authenticator app)
Everything here uses sample data. Nothing is sent to customers and nothing is written to CoreBridge.
If you click around and want a clean slate: Admin Settings > Company > Reset Sample Data.

## 1. Activities (your home screen after sign-in)
- To Do groups: Missing Next Step, Past Due, Due Today, Due Soon, Due Later. Colored chips filter by kind.
- Click Done on a task: Today's Scorecard counts it. Snooze moves it 1 day, 3 days or a week. Preview 🎉 shows the celebration.
- + New Task can be assigned to a teammate (Admin > Team Members).
- + New Task, or Add Next Step on a Missing Next Step row (it pre-picks the project).
- Right side: Coming Up Today · No Surprises, Handled For You Today, Proofs Needing a Nudge.
- Hand-off tasks (install, ship, pickup) appear by themselves when every item on a project is built.
- Account menu (your initials): switch location, pick a theme, Preview SalesMax As Design/Production/Permits/Purchasing.

## 2. Projects
- Tiles: On schedule · Stuck · Missing a next step · Lost pipeline · Open pipeline.
- + New (top bar) > New Project or Prospect. A prospect and a project are one record.
- Open "Patio menu boards" (an estimate): Customer Said Yes? > record deposit, PO or terms > it moves to In production.
- Open "Lobby sign and desk logo": Where This Stands (✦), Customer Flags, Next Steps, Customer PO check, History. Cards show Stuck / No next step badges.
- Prospects and estimates can be Marked as Lost with a reason (feeds Lost pipeline).

## 3. Customers
- Customers to Keep Warm (from Admin day counts), Download CSV.
- Riverbend Dental: Insight ✦ with 👍/👎, Upcoming Needs (becomes a task on that date), tabs with counts, Contacts (+ Add Contact), Activity log, Account tab last (Classification, Shipping Accounts Express/Ground/Freight).

## 4. Boards
- Design and Production: Board/List, Columns, moves.
- Permits: drag a card between columns; Permit Details (Application #, Permit #, Issued, Expires). Approved needs a Permit #.
- Purchasing: Check Vendor Quote. Try quantity 2 on "Wall graphic print media" (we sold 1): it's blocked and can't be ordered.

## 5. Customer order page (what your customer sees)
- From a project item: Send Proof > the link opens the customer page.
- Most likely ready date (and No later than), Enter Your PO (shows up on the project's PO check), Pay panel (card fee from Admin), Approve New Price (+$45.00), + Add Comment, proof approval checklist.
- Proof versions are Google Drive links (Send Proof page > + Add New Proof Version).

## 5b. Public pages
- Request a Quote: /request (no sign-in). It creates a Prospect and a "QR-0001" quote task on your Activities.
- Estimates on the customer order page can be approved online (name + agree). The project moves to In production and you get a Collect the deposit task.

## 6. Admin Settings (initials menu)
- Six tabs, Advanced sections, status strip. Price approval threshold, deposit %, card fee, reminders, review link, estimate notes, goals.
- Workflow Builder: edit owners/days as a draft, or type to the Process Expert ("Give quality control 2 days"), then Publish.
- Set Up Workflow: the 8-question Process Expert interview, then Looks Right · Build My Workflow fills the draft.
- Team Members, Import From CoreBridge (off until you allow it).
- Nightly Reconciliation, Past Due (reminder drafts + pay links), Moving Off CoreBridge checklist, Change Log.

## 6b. New this morning (your three asks)
- Email (top nav): Archive, Mark as Spam (blocks that sender), Not Spam, Out of Office auto-reply. There is no delete anywhere.
- Project > Hand-Off & Address: Pickup, Install, Deliver or Ship with the address. It prints on the estimate and Invoice Preview.
- Project shipped by XPS: + Add XPS Label (cost + tracking). Invoice adds cost + markup (Admin > Pricing). Moving an item to Complete is blocked until you confirm the shipping charge is on the CoreBridge order.

## 7. Other
- Performance (Preview 🎆 fireworks), Team (stuck work + escalations), Field (site visit/install checklists; photos to Google Drive), Search, Feedback, My Profile (Morning Summary preview), Billing & Add-ons, Help.
